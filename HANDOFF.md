## What Doodle is

A recursively zoomable creative document. On the field it is a node graph — Model → Prompt / Negative → Image Generator → Preview — in The Soft Machine. Every node can be **entered**: a written thing (page, scene, note) becomes a **document**; a **place** (a chapter) becomes a **field of what it holds**. So a book is chapters, a chapter is a field of pages, a page is a sheet you write on with beats and notes inside — and **zoom crosses the levels** (`WRITTEN`/`PLACES` in `kinds.ts`; `ENTER_AT 2.2`, `LEAVE_AT 0.18` in `Canvas.tsx`; one level per gesture). Four base workflows are templates: **Images, Film, Manga, Book**. The agent (ChatGPT via Codex, Ollama, or a mock) proposes; the user keeps.

## Decisions that hold

- Vite + React + TypeScript in front of a Tauri 2 crate. Port 1430 (Orb is 1420).
- Our own canvas engine, no React Flow: a world layer under floating chrome — moved by a `translate`, grown by CSS **`zoom`** (`.world-scale`), not `scale()`, so cards re-lay out at the magnification and type and hairlines stay crisp — SVG wires in world units.
- The graph is JSON in a folder — `Name.doodle/graph.json` + `assets/` + `backups/` + `jobs.json`. SQLite only when objects/relationships/search need it.
- The work is in-window pills; the boring is on the native menu bar (`src-tauri/src/menu.rs` → ids → `src/platform/menu.ts`).
- Providers behind one port (`src/providers/types.ts`): **mock** (real infrastructure — latency, steps, cancel, streaming, a fixed shot list, the fixture varied by seed), **Ollama** (text; available only with a writer-family model — `qwen3:14b` is installed), **ChatGPT through Codex** (text on the app server, images through `codex exec` and the CLI's image tool). Who draws / who writes is chosen on the Queue chevron and remembered (`ui.drawWith / writeWith`); node selects name checkpoints/models only.
- Signal is **Rams amber** — an orange-yellow, `--signal-hsl: 39 90% 51%` (`#f4a41a`) — on the play, on every wire (`hsl(var(--signal-hsl) / var(--wire))`) and on a connected port's dot: what is wired is what will run. **Roles** (Doodle's amendment to doctrine 8): one dusty hue per kind in the amber's own Braun register (chroma 12–50%, strength from the wash `0.22/0.36`, not the hue) — model slate, scene blue, note sand, shot clay, character rose, style violet, generator teal, writer indigo, preview/page olive — `--role-*: h s l` in `app.css`, worn by `.k-<kind>` (`--role`) and `.t-<type>` (`--flow`: model slate, text blue, image green). Always a ground (a wash, a dot, a wire, a tile), never type, never state. Dark default; light must hold. Icon: DD in Silkscreen on charcoal.
- Class names: `.field`, `.doc`, `.page`, `.sec`, `.head`, `.sheet` are taken (system or shell) — `.sheet` cost a card its `position: absolute`. Check `soft-machine.css` and `app.css` before naming; the canvas surface is `.stage` (`.stage.reading` for a page).

## How it works, by area

**Window.** Overlay title bar, lights at `{20,32}` (rows centre on y 30). Head: `‹ tab ›` — the arrows step to the previous/next **sibling of the same kind** of the entered node (`nav.siblings/sibling/step`, View › Previous/Next, ⇧⌘[ ⇧⌘]) — with the trail when entered (`Untitled film › Scene 08`), `⋮`, the amber **Queue** split pill (run · chevron menu), `×` stop, duplicate, `≡` panes. Panes float as cards at content height: **Outline** (`shell/Navigator.tsx`: the whole document as a tree the way Blender's outliner shows a scene — the book, then chapters, pages, what they hold; twist folds; the way down to here is always unfolded; click goes there — that level shown, it selected and framed; double-click enters; click a chosen row again to **rename** in place; **drag** a row before/after another or into it (`graph.reorder`: order is `seq`, siblings renumbered; a new parent gives it a spot to the right on that field); `+` adds under what is chosen — a chapter at the book, a page in a chapter, a beat in a scene, a note in a page; rejected rows dim; the tree takes the **keyboard** when clicked into: arrows walk, fold and unfold and go up, **⌘↑ ⌘↓** move a row among its own, ⏎ enters, F2 renames) and **Inspector** (state segment; the node's fields from the kind registry; the **Bible** — tone/rules/avoid — when nothing is selected at the root). Foot: **you** — a round mark (`boring-avatars`, bauhaus, seeded `WHO` in `Foot.tsx`, a placeholder until a picture is set) → Settings (General: shortcuts, motion; Appearance: light/dark/system; Providers: who is up; About), readouts `T I N`, the view cluster (fit · reveal · lens │ − % +). The bar at the foot is one plate: a mono line naming whose words and where they go (`Scene 08 → Storyboard`, the role's dot), the words growing to four lines, then quiet keys (runs, save), the note, and one round amber key — run, or stop while it runs; on a page it is the **ask** with the same key. On the field **Enter runs and puts the bar away** (`ui.bar`, never remembered; × puts it away too), shift-Enter breaks a line; away, it is one quiet round key at the foot (`.bar-key`), and **/** or the key brings it back focused with the caret at the end (also ⌘K → The prompt bar). On a page the ask is the same key until opened (`ui.ask`, `/` or the key; Escape or × closes). A chapter's field gets the prompt bar for its own first runnable. `nav.reading(focus, read)` is the one answer to "is this a document?". **Read** (`canvas/Read.tsx`, the head's book key on a place, `ui.read`, never remembered; Escape leaves it first; it puts the bar away): a place — the book, a chapter — as one column: title, then chapter by chapter, page by page in order, every page a `Prose` written in where it lies with its name as a folio.

**Field** (`canvas/Canvas.tsx`). Pan (trackpad, Space-drag, middle), pinch/⌘-wheel zoom (in past 2.2 over a written thing enters it; out past 0.18 inside something rises with the thing left framed; on a document a pinch out rises), fixed zoom stops, fit between the panes, marquee, shift-select, drag with 8 px snap on drop, arrows nudge, ⌥-drop into a card, right-click menus (node: Open/Run/Duplicate/State/Delete; field: Add here/Fit), double-click empty → Add here, `›` open key on hover, Enter/Escape enter and rise, L or the cluster key = lens (unrelated fades), below 0.4 zoom = the map (`.lod-map`: title-only cards, ports on the band; `setMapMode` in `layout.ts`).

**Cards** (`canvas/Node.tsx`, `layout.ts`). Head band in the role wash with a full dot and the name; the **output** is one dot on the band's right edge (`HEAD_H 30`); **inputs** are 24 px rows from `ROWS_TOP 36` down the left edge — dot, word, and *what feeds it* by name when wired; then the kind's rows; ports carry `data-port="node:port"` / `data-dir` and `.t-<type>` (the type still colours an unconnected dot's ring and the lighting while a wire is out). Wires (`Wires.tsx`) are the signal. A wire that **crosses a level** is drawn as far as it can be seen: dashed into the band of the card that holds its other end (`resolve()` climbs to the card at this level), or as a short dashed **lead** with the far end's name in mono (`.wire-from`) when it comes from beyond this level; both ends inside one card = not drawn. While a wire is out, landable inputs light and it snaps to the nearest within 40 px; let go on the field → `Offer` (one row per kind that takes it, made there, wired); **C** connects two selected; double-click cuts.

**Kinds** (`graph/kinds.ts`): model, prompt, generate, preview, character, style, write, page, note, shot, **chapter** — ports, size, default data, inspector groups. A **page** and a **chapter** are **paper** on the field (`.node.leaf`: `--paper`, a hairline `--paper-edge` as a cut sheet — the one edge on content, deliberate; a **running head** in small mono instead of a band — `PAGE 1 · FROM WRITE` — with the one input on the head's left edge (`portPos`) and no input rows; 20 px margins; the count as a folio): the page is its words, written on in place at 13.5/1.7, 300×400; the chapter is its pages as a small stack with the count, and its summary. A chapter's `text` output is its pages in order (`describe()`); its prose key is `summary`. The navigator's `+` inside a chapter lays the next page to the right. Templates in `graph/templates.ts`. Every node has `parent` (entering), `seq` (creation order), `status` (canon / draft / exploration / rejected — rejected never counts as context, wears the word, steps back).

**Pages** (`canvas/Doc.tsx`). Entered, a written node is a page — the writing mode: the stage takes a calmer ground (`--reading`), one column at a 680 px measure set between the panes (`.win.nav-on/.ins-on` pad the `.docpage`), a mono meta line with the role's dot (`PAGE · IN ONE · 37 WORDS`, Run for a runnable), the title inline at 32 px (renames), the words at 16.5/1.75 with the caret already in them at the end; a page ends in its **turn** (`Turn`: ‹ previous · n of N · next ›, or **+ New page** on the last, made beside it and entered); what it holds and its media wait below as quiet one-line sections (`.paper-below`, the `+` on hover). Then its body — prose for prompt/note/page; character sheet; style sheet (palette as chips); generator = contact sheet of takes (ringed = the take) + settings + runs + its own Run; preview = the print; writer = output + settings; shot = description + Camera group; then **Beats/Notes** (children as a numbered list written in place, `›` opens) and **Media**. `@` in prose opens the graph's names (`canvas/mentions.tsx`); `expandMentions` turns `@Title` into the thing's description in every request. Select words → **Beat from selection** (a note on a page), and the child is **tied** to the passage (`state/anchors.ts`: the anchor is the quoted words plus the offset they were taken at, never an index). `locate()` finds them again to the letter, then ignoring how the whitespace fell, then by their first and last four words — so a tie survives inserting before it and editing inside it, and says **adrift** when the passage is gone rather than pointing somewhere wrong. The page marks tied passages under the words (`.prose-marks`, a sand underline; lit when the pointer is on the beat), and a beat's row says where it came from with **untie** / **re-tie** (re-tie takes the next passage you select).

**The agent.** `state/drafts.ts`: Expand / Continue / Rewrite / free ask → a **draft** on the page, streamed word by word when the provider can (`streamText`), Stop cancels; Keep appends into the kind's words (`proseKey`; Replace for rewrite), discard drops. `state/shots.ts`: **Shots** on a scene → six under `SHOTS_SCHEMA` (Codex honours `outputSchema`; Ollama takes it as `format`; mock returns its list) → **Proposed shots** ghosts → Keep / Keep all / drop → `shot` nodes inside the scene. `bibleText()` and mentions ride into every request; rejected nodes never do.

**Codex** (`src-tauri/src/codex.rs`, `providers/codex.ts`). Finds the binary (`DOODLE_CODEX`, Homebrew, `/Applications/ChatGPT.app/Contents/Resources/codex`, `~/.local/bin`), reads only `auth_mode` from `~/.codex/auth.json`. **Text:** one long-lived `codex app-server` child, JSON-RPC over stdio (`initialize`/`initialized`; per turn a fresh ephemeral read-only thread, `approvalPolicy: never`; `turn/start` with prompt + optional `outputSchema`; `turn/interrupt`); the reader thread emits one Tauri event `codex` (`{kind: delta|done|failed, turnId, …}`, camelCase via `rename_all_fields`). **Images:** `codex exec --ephemeral -s workspace-write` in a scratch folder under the app cache, the CLI's `image_generation` tool, newest PNG = result (cannot be cancelled mid-flight). `DOODLE_CODEX_TRACE=1` prints every server line. Protocol schema for the installed version: `codex app-server generate-json-schema --out <dir>`. Each call carries Codex's preamble (~10–15k tokens). Verified 2026-09-21: `chatgpt · plus`, streaming, schema, images.

**Provenance** (`state/prov.ts`). Every generated thing records where it came from — provider, model, when, the prompt and system as they went out, the seed, what fed it (node, port and title as they were), the bible's rules, the run — keyed by **what it produced**: an image by its asset reference (content-addressed; `rekey()` follows them when the first save moves them into the folder), words by the run's id, which the writer and the pages it wrote carry as `node.from`. It saves inside `graph.json` (`provenance`) and comes back with the document. The inspector shows it as **Where this came from** for whatever is selected (`behind()` resolves through a node's take or its `from`): who and when, the seed, the inputs as rows that select them, and *what was asked* folded away.

**Jobs** (`state/jobs.ts`). One at a time; `requestFor`/`textRequestFor` read through the wires (scene, then character, then style, then the bible); a generator renders `count` candidates (next seed along each), outputs to `assets/` via `write_asset` when the graph has a home, bloom under the rows, the take flows on; a **writer streams** (`job.partial`, shown on the page it feeds — card and document — as *arriving*, read-only, never the document until the end) and then **lays its words on pages** (`graph.layOnPages`: `paginate()` cuts at `PAGE_WORDS` 350 by paragraph, then by sentence; the page it feeds first, then the empty pages after it, then new pages made to the right — a page with its own words is never written over). The mock writer gives twelve paragraphs for `Length: Chapter`, one sentence for `Beat`; History (bar clock) lists runs, retries failed ones. Runs persist in `jobs.json`; in-flight at close → cancelled.

**Disk** (`state/doc.ts`, `src-tauri/src/commands.rs`). Save asks where once, then autosaves 600 ms after every journal entry; **Export as Markdown** (File › ⇧⌘E, ⌘K): the place you are in — the book, or the chapter — in reading order, chapters as headings, empty pages left out (`state/reading.ts` is the one order, shared with Read; `write_text` in Rust, atomic), then revealed in the Finder; atomic writes; `backups/` before overwrite (≤1 per 10 min, newest 10); Duplicate Graph, Reveal in Finder; last graph restored on launch with its views. Assets hashed into `assets/<sha>.<ext>`; read back as data URLs (no asset-protocol scope); drops from Finder land on the card under the pointer or the open page. CSP set (`tauri.conf.json`; looser `devCsp`).

**Journal** (`state/history.ts`). Snapshot entries; a drag is one, a run of typing is one (coalesce by key ≤1 s); undo/redo; selection survives. Edit menu items are Doodle's own so the journal gets them.

**Search.** ⌘K palette: nodes by title/kind/words + run/new/fit/settings; Enter frames, ⌘Enter opens. ⌘/ shortcuts sheet.

## Working

`pnpm tauri dev` (Rust ~1 min first time; `DOODLE_CODEX_TRACE=1 pnpm tauri dev` to watch Codex). The webview sometimes holds a half-applied HMR — `touch index.html` forces a reload. Capture only when Doodle is frontmost: front it with System Events, find the window by CoreGraphics (a 10-line Swift `win` listing `CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo` for owner `doodle`), `screencapture -R` its frame; keys via System Events `keystroke`; clicks via a CGEvent `click x y` (System Events `click at` is refused). Drive the app read-only; leave file dialogs to the user. The web view has no working `window.confirm` (it answers no at once) — ask through `confirmAsk()` in `platform/fs.ts` (the dialog plugin's `ask`). `http://localhost:1430` in a browser works for pointer tests (⌘ keys mapped in `devKeys`); the Tauri `listen` error there is harmless; the browser tool's Return key does not reach the page — dispatch a KeyboardEvent instead.

## Known

- Inert: the cluster's reveal key.
- The mock writer repeats the brief before its paragraph.
- Outputs rendered before a graph is saved are data URLs until the first save moves them.
- The pane state once flipped to off/off during HMR + a process swap; never on a clean launch; store key is `doodle.ui.v1`. After many HMR patches the webview can hold stale handlers (keys, the wheel) — the fix is a relaunch, not code. Nothing may focus a field on arrival (the bar once did, and every field key went into the prompt).

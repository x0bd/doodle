## What Doodle is

A recursively zoomable creative document. On the field it is a node graph — Model → Prompt / Negative → Image Generator → Preview — in The Soft Machine. Every node can be **entered**: a written thing (page, scene, note) becomes a **document**; a **place** (a chapter) becomes a **field of what it holds**. So a book is chapters, a chapter is a field of pages, a page is a sheet you write on with beats and notes inside — and **zoom crosses the levels** (`WRITTEN`/`PLACES` in `kinds.ts`; `ENTER_AT 2.2`, `LEAVE_AT 0.18` in `Canvas.tsx`; one level per gesture). Four base workflows are templates: **Images, Film, Manga, Book**. The agent (ChatGPT via Codex, Ollama, or a mock) proposes; the user keeps.

## Decisions that hold

- Vite + React + TypeScript in front of a Tauri 2 crate. Port 1430 (Orb is 1420).
- Our own canvas engine, no React Flow: a world layer under floating chrome — moved by a `translate`, grown by CSS **`zoom`** (`.world-scale`), not `scale()`, so cards re-lay out at the magnification and type and hairlines stay crisp — SVG wires in world units.
- The graph is JSON in a folder — `Name.doodle/graph.json` + `assets/` + `backups/` + `jobs.json`. SQLite only when objects/relationships/search need it.
- The work is in-window pills; the boring is on the native menu bar (`src-tauri/src/menu.rs` → ids → `src/platform/menu.ts`). That listener belongs to the **window**, not to a component (kept on `window.__doodleMenu`, never removed): a long run of hot patches over `App` once left the platform's own menu talking to nobody, and ⌘N looked broken when nothing was.
- Providers behind one port (`src/providers/types.ts`): **mock** (real infrastructure — latency, steps, cancel, streaming, a fixed shot list, the fixture varied by seed), **Ollama** (text; available only with a writer-family model — `qwen3:14b` is installed), **ChatGPT through Codex** (text on the app server, images through `codex exec` and the CLI's image tool). Who draws / who writes is chosen on the Queue chevron and remembered (`ui.drawWith / writeWith`); node selects name checkpoints/models only.
- Signal is **Rams amber** — an orange-yellow, `--signal-hsl: 39 90% 51%` (`#f4a41a`) — on the play, on every wire (`hsl(var(--signal-hsl) / var(--wire))`) and on a connected port's dot: what is wired is what will run. **Roles** (Doodle's amendment to doctrine 8): one dusty hue per kind in the amber's own Braun register (chroma 12–50%, strength from the wash `0.22/0.36`, not the hue) — model slate, scene blue, note sand, shot clay, character rose, location khaki, style violet, generator teal, writer indigo, preview/page olive — `--role-*: h s l` in `app.css`, worn by `.k-<kind>` (`--role`) and `.t-<type>` (`--flow`: model slate, text blue, image green). Always a ground (a wash, a dot, a wire, a tile), never type, never state. Dark default; light must hold. Icon: DD in Silkscreen on charcoal.
- Class names: `.field`, `.doc`, `.page`, `.sec`, `.head`, `.sheet` are taken (system or shell) — `.sheet` cost a card its `position: absolute`. Check `soft-machine.css` and `app.css` before naming; the canvas surface is `.stage` (`.stage.reading` for a page).

## How it works, by area

**Window.** Overlay title bar, lights at `{20,32}` (rows centre on y 30). Head: `‹ tab ›` — the arrows step to the previous/next **sibling of the same kind** of the entered node (`nav.siblings/sibling/step`, View › Previous/Next, ⇧⌘[ ⇧⌘]) — with the trail when entered (`Untitled film › Scene 08`), the **Read** key (a book, on a place only), the amber **Queue** split pill (run · chevron menu), `×` stop, duplicate, `≡` panes. Panes float as cards at content height: **Outline** (`shell/Navigator.tsx`: the whole document as a tree the way Blender's outliner shows a scene — the book, then chapters, pages, what they hold; twist folds; the way down to here is always unfolded; click goes there — that level shown, it selected and framed; double-click enters; click a chosen row again to **rename** in place; **drag** a row before/after another or into it (`graph.reorder`: order is `seq`, siblings renumbered; a new parent gives it a spot to the right on that field); `+` adds under what is chosen — a chapter at the book, a page in a chapter, a beat in a scene, a note in a page; rejected rows dim; the tree takes the **keyboard** when clicked into: arrows walk, fold and unfold and go up, **⌘↑ ⌘↓** move a row among its own, ⏎ enters, F2 renames) and **Inspector** (state segment; the node's fields from the kind registry; the **Bible** — tone/rules/avoid — when nothing is selected at the root). Foot: **you** — a round mark (`boring-avatars`, bauhaus, seeded `WHO` in `Foot.tsx`, a placeholder until a picture is set) → Settings (General: shortcuts, motion; Appearance: light/dark/system; Providers: who is up; About), readouts `T I N`, the view cluster (fit · **the map** · lens │ − % +). The bar at the foot is one plate: a mono line naming whose words and where they go (`Scene 08 → Storyboard`, the role's dot), the words growing to four lines, then quiet keys (runs, save), the note, and one round amber key — run, or stop while it runs; on a page it is the **ask** with the same key. On the field **Enter runs and puts the bar away** (`ui.bar`, never remembered; × puts it away too), shift-Enter breaks a line; away, it is one quiet round key at the foot (`.bar-key`), and **/** or the key brings it back focused with the caret at the end (also ⌘K → The prompt bar). On a page the ask is the same key until opened (`ui.ask`, `/` or the key; Escape or × closes). A chapter's field gets the prompt bar for its own first runnable. `nav.reading(focus, read)` is the one answer to "is this a document?". **Read** (`canvas/Read.tsx`, the head's book key on a place, `ui.read`, never remembered; Escape leaves it first; it puts the bar away): a place — the book, a chapter — as one column: title, then chapter by chapter, page by page in order, every page a `Prose` written in where it lies with its name as a folio.

**Field** (`canvas/Canvas.tsx`). Pan (trackpad, Space-drag, middle), pinch/⌘-wheel zoom (in past 2.2 over a written thing enters it; out past 0.18 inside something rises with the thing left framed; on a document a pinch out rises), fixed zoom stops, fit between the panes, marquee, shift-select, drag with 8 px snap on drop, arrows nudge, ⌥-drop into a card, right-click menus (node: Open/Run/Duplicate/State/Delete; field: Add here/Fit), double-click empty → Add here, `›` open key on hover, Enter/Escape enter and rise, L or the cluster key = lens (unrelated fades), below 0.4 zoom = the **far view** (`.lod-map`: title-only cards, ports on the band; `setMapMode` in `layout.ts`). **The map** (`shell/Minimap.tsx`, the cluster's eye or **M**, remembered): this level whole in the corner — every card its role's wash, the window's own view a slab of tint; press or drag on it to look there.

**Cards** (`canvas/Node.tsx`, `layout.ts`). Read back against the doctrine, not against a screenshot (see *Taste* below); drawn as the platform would draw its own objects in The Soft Machine's terms — the system's lit lip and fallen shade, lifted by a two-light shadow (`--card-lift`, `--card-lift-2`: a close contact and a long soft fall), the name at the headline size (13/600). **Head:** a band in the role's wash, a little brighter at the top than the bottom, a **lit dot** (`--lit-*`, the roles at full chroma, worn only by small round marks — a card's dot, the bar's, a page's meta, a lineage row, the map's selection) and the name; the **output** is one **jack** on the band's right edge (`HEAD_H 36`). State is a sentence-case word in a capsule of tint (*Draft*). **Inputs:** 26 px rows (`PORT_ROW 26`) from `ROWS_TOP 42` down the left edge — a jack, the port's name, and *what feeds it* as a **token**: a capsule in the source's own wash with its lit dot, the way a variable sits in a sentence. `layout.ts` and the CSS share these numbers; when they drifted (24 vs 26) the plugs hung above their jacks. A **jack** is a ring cut into the card's edge, dark, with a thin collar in what flows through it (`.t-<type>`: model slate, text blue, image green); patched, the collar is amber. Ports carry `data-port="node:port"` / `data-dir`. **Body:** what the card is — prompts and notes a slot of words that grows with them (`field-sizing: content`, to 260 px), a model a pop-up, a **preview** its print to the card's edges and corners (*Nothing yet* until there is one), a **character** a contact card (the face in a round, or their initials on their role until there is one), a **location** a map card (the view across the top, 16:9, the role's hatch until there is a picture), a generator ends in its bloom of takes; the kinds with settings (generate, write, style, shot) show **the fields the kind declares, live** (`Form` → the inspector's own `FieldRow`: one registry, two places). **Nothing on a card is drawn** (doctrine 1): no row plates, no dividers, no rule under a group's name — a group is a word (`.node-rule`) with `--gap-near` above it. Labels Inter, figures mono (doctrine 7). Controls keep the capsule and wear the **tint** when chosen (doctrine 5): a **slider** is a thin track filled in the role's lit tone with the platform's round white knob and its figure beside it (`-webkit-appearance: none` on the input and the thumb, or WebKit draws its own); a **segmented** choice is a track of tint with the chosen segment the card's own face lifted out of it; a stepper is the same track with the figure between its keys; a chooser is a **pop-up** — its word and the up-and-down chevrons (`PopUpIcon`, here and in the inspector); something written into is a slot. A row whose control will not fit beside its name gives it the line below, against the far edge. On a card everything is **flat**; the tactile versions of the same controls live in the panes.

**Wires are patch leads** (`Wires.tsx`): amber, **hanging** between their ends (`wirePath` sags with the run, to 30 px, flat in the far view), each end a **plug** seated in its jack (`.wire-plug`); the cables feeding a running node carry a marching dash (`.wire.working`, still for reduced motion). A wire that **crosses a level** is drawn as far as it can be seen: dashed into the band of the card that holds its other end (`resolve()` climbs to the card at this level), or as a short dashed **lead** named in mono (`.wire-from`) when it comes from beyond this level; both ends inside one card = not drawn. While a wire is out, landable inputs light and it snaps to the nearest within 40 px; let go on the field → `Offer` (one row per kind that takes it, made there, wired); **C** connects two selected; double-click cuts.

**Kinds** (`graph/kinds.ts`): model, prompt, generate, preview, character, **location**, style, write, page, note, shot, chapter — ports, a starting size, default data, the fields (`line`, `text`, `select`, `number`, `range`, `choice`, `seed`). A node can also be **given inputs by hand** (`node.extras`, `graph.addInput/dropInput`): right-click a writer or a generator → **Give it** → a character, a place, a style, a scene, a note, a shot — the card is made to its left, the port appears, the wire is drawn, and what it holds rides into every request (`given()` in `jobs.ts`); *Tidy the empty ones* takes back the ports nothing feeds. A card is **as tall as what it holds**: nothing sets a height, the kinds that would collapse carry a `min-height` on their body in `app.css`, and a `ResizeObserver` tells the engine what the browser laid out (`graph.measure`, `offsetHeight`, which CSS `zoom` does not scale — no journal entry, no dirty document) so bounds, fits, marquees and drops agree with the screen. **Width** is set by hand: the grip on a card's right edge (`.node-grip`, double-click restores the kind's own), journalled and coalesced per card. A **page** and a **chapter** are **paper** on the field (`.node.leaf`: `--paper`, a hairline `--paper-edge` as a cut sheet — the one edge on content, deliberate; a **running head** instead of a band — *Page 1  from Write*, small, in Inter — with the one input on the head's left edge (`portPos`) and no input rows; 20 px margins; the foot carries the count and the **folio**, the page's place among its own): the page is its words, written on in place at 13.5/1.7, 300×400; the chapter is a sheet with **two more stacked under it** (box-shadows, `--paper-under`), its name at the headline weight, its summary, and its **contents** the way a book lists them — each page's name, how it opens, its number — five, then *and n more*, and the count. A chapter's `text` output is its pages in order (`describe()`); its prose key is `summary`. The navigator's `+` inside a chapter lays the next page to the right. Templates in `graph/templates.ts` (the Film's Storyboard has a `place` port given by hand, fed by *The greenhouse*, laid under the Model so it clears the style card — cards are as tall as they hold, so a template spaces for the grown height, not the kind's `size.h`; the Book is a cast, a voice and two chapters of pages). Every node has `parent` (entering), `seq` (creation order), `status` (canon / draft / exploration / rejected — rejected never counts as context, wears the word, steps back).

**Pages** (`canvas/Doc.tsx`). Entered, a written node is a page — the writing mode: the stage takes a calmer ground (`--reading`), one column at a 680 px measure set between the panes (`.win.nav-on/.ins-on` pad the `.docpage`), a mono meta line with the role's dot (`PAGE · IN ONE · 37 WORDS`, Run for a runnable), a **Prose | Screenplay** switch (`data.form`), and ≈ running time for a screenplay (180 words a page, a page a minute), the title inline at 32 px (renames), the words at 16.5/1.75 in **the writer** (below) with the caret already in them at the end; a page ends in its **turn** (`Turn`: ‹ previous · n of N · next ›, or **+ New page** on the last, made beside it and entered); what it holds and its media wait below as quiet one-line sections (`.paper-below`, the `+` on hover). Then its body — prose for prompt/note/page; character sheet; style sheet (palette as chips); generator = contact sheet of takes (ringed = the take) + settings + runs + its own Run; preview = the print; writer = output + settings; shot = description + Camera group; then **Beats/Notes** (children as a numbered list written in place, `›` opens) and **Media**. `@` in prose opens the graph's names (the writer's menu, fed by `mentionables()` in `canvas/mentions.tsx`), and a name after `@` reads as a token; `expandMentions` turns `@Title` into the thing's description in every request. Select words → the bubble's **+ Beat** (**+ Note** on a page), and the child is **tied** to the passage (`state/anchors.ts`: the anchor is the quoted words plus the offset they were taken at in the **plain** words — `plain()`, the markup read away — never an index, so bolding a passage never loosens its tie). `locate()` finds them again to the letter, then ignoring how the whitespace fell, then by their first and last four words — so a tie survives inserting before it and editing inside it, and says **adrift** when the passage is gone rather than pointing somewhere wrong. The writer marks tied passages under the words (decorations, `.tie`, a sand underline; lit when the pointer is on the beat), and a beat's row says where it came from with **untie** / **re-tie** (re-tie takes the next passage you select).

**The writer** (`src/writer/`). ProseMirror (`schema.ts`, `Editor.tsx`), in every place words are written — the page in writing mode, a page's sheet on the field, Read, a note, a prompt. **The words stay text** in `node.data.text`, so the prompts, pagination, Read, the export and anchors go on reading words: prose is a small **Markdown** (`#`/`##`/`###`, `>` quotes, `**strong**`, `_em_` — `*em*` read too, and written inside a word — `~~strike~~`, a line break inside a paragraph, a blank line between paragraphs); a screenplay is a small **Fountain** (a scene heading starts INT./EXT. or is forced with `.`, a character in capitals with dialogue on the lines under it, a parenthetical in brackets, a transition ends in TO: or is forced with `>`, the rest action; `!` forces action). `markup.ts` reads and writes both, escapes what would otherwise read as markup, and keeps a delimiter from ever touching a space on its inside (`tidy`) — the plan (§8.3) wanted ProseMirror JSON as canonical; text was chosen because Markdown and Fountain are what a writer, a model and an export already read, and it spares every consumer a second format. `pnpm test` fuzzes it: 1,000 random documents per form through write → read → write → read, the document and its words unchanged (5,000 were run once). Typing: `## ` a heading, `> ` a quote, `**…**`/`*…*`/`~~…~~` marks as you close them; ⌘B ⌘I ⇧⌘X; ⇧⌘T/H/J/B title, heading, subheading, body (Notes' own keys); ⇧↩ a line break. In a screenplay: `INT. ` makes a scene heading, Enter moves on the way the trade does (scene → action, character → dialogue, dialogue → action, transition → scene; Enter on an empty element steps back to action), Tab turns the block (action → character → transition → scene; dialogue ⇄ parenthetical), `(` in dialogue a parenthetical, `… TO:` a transition. Around the words: a **bubble** over a selection (B I S, and the page's + Beat), a quiet **key in the margin** naming the caret's block with a menu to change it (hidden beside the Outline on a narrow window), `@` tokens. The editor keeps its own **history**: Edit › Undo/Redo go to the writer with the keyboard (`editorUndo` in `platform/menu.ts`) before the journal; a change from elsewhere (a writer streaming, the journal) is read in again with the caret kept where it was in the words. An image model reads the words **bare** (`describe(n, true)` in `jobs.ts`); a writer reads them as written.

**The agent.** `state/drafts.ts`: Expand / Continue / Rewrite / free ask → a **draft** on the page, streamed word by word when the provider can (`streamText`), Stop cancels; Keep appends into the kind's words (`proseKey`; Replace for rewrite), discard drops. `state/shots.ts`: **Shots** on a scene → six under `SHOTS_SCHEMA` (Codex honours `outputSchema`; Ollama takes it as `format`; mock returns its list) → **Proposed shots** ghosts → Keep / Keep all / drop → `shot` nodes inside the scene. `bibleText()` and mentions ride into every request; rejected nodes never do.

**Doodle as an MCP server** (`src-tauri/src/mcp.rs`, `src/agent/tools.ts`, `state/ideas.ts`) — the agent works the document with tools (plan §9, §11.3: **observe and propose**, never mutate). A Streamable-HTTP MCP endpoint on `127.0.0.1:<port the system picks>/mcp` (`tiny_http`), behind a bearer token made fresh each launch (a request without it: 401); it only speaks the protocol and hands `tools/list` and `tools/call` to the webview as a `mcp` event, answered with `mcp_reply` (30 s). Codex gets it **per thread**, never through `~/.codex/config.toml`: `thread/start` → `config.mcp_servers.doodle = { url, http_headers: { Authorization }, default_tools_approval_mode: "approve", … }` plus `developerInstructions` (`briefing()`). Why `approve`: under `approvalPolicy: never` an MCP call is otherwise refused (*"MCP tool call requires approval"*); the modes are `auto | prompt | writes | approve`, found by passing a wrong one. The tools: `doodle_here` (what is open and selected, the path, the bible), `doodle_outline` (the tree with kinds, ids, states, sizes), `doodle_read` (a node whole — words as written, fields, what feeds it, what is inside, tied passages), `doodle_find`; and `propose_beats` (with `quote`: a kept beat is tied to that passage), `propose_shots` (into the shot proposals), `propose_text` (a draft, replace or append), `propose_characters`, `propose_places` — **ideas** are ghosts on the page (`state/ideas.ts`, Keep / Keep all / drop, one journal entry). A **free ask** on a page carries the tools (`drafts.ts`): the draft's head says what the agent is doing (*ChatGPT · reading…*), and when it used them its reply is an account, read and dismissed, never kept (`reply`); what it proposed waits on its own. The **mock is an agent too** — it calls the same functions directly (`req.runTool`: here → read → propose a beat per sentence, tied; *shots* or *characters* in the ask for those), so the whole loop runs for free. Ollama has no tools yet (it answers in words). One listener per window (`__doodleAgent`, the previous one gone before the next arrives — React's development double mount once left two, and every tool ran twice). Codex's reader emits `message` when the agent speaks again after a tool, so the draft shows only its latest words. Checked on 2026-09-23 with ChatGPT: *"Propose three beats for this panel"* → it read the page and proposed three beats, each quoting its passage.

**Codex** (`src-tauri/src/codex.rs`, `providers/codex.ts`). Finds **the newest** binary on the machine (`find()`: `DOODLE_CODEX` wins outright; otherwise every candidate — `/opt/homebrew/bin`, `/usr/local/bin`, npm's native binary inside `lib/node_modules/@openai/codex` (never its Node launcher: an app opened from the Finder may have no `node` on its PATH), the ChatGPT app's own, `~/.local/bin` — is asked `--version` and the highest wins, a release over a prerelease; found once per launch), reads only `auth_mode` from `~/.codex/auth.json`. Why newest: the account's default model moves with the service and an older CLI is refused it (*"The 'gpt-6-astra' model requires a newer version of Codex"* — the Homebrew cask was 0.149.1 and stale on 2026-09-23 and was removed; `npm install -g @openai/codex@latest` gave 0.156.1, which is what runs now). Keep it current with that command. **Text:** one long-lived `codex app-server` child, JSON-RPC over stdio (`initialize`/`initialized`; per turn a fresh ephemeral read-only thread, `approvalPolicy: never`; `turn/start` with prompt + optional `outputSchema`; `turn/interrupt`); the reader thread emits one Tauri event `codex` (`{kind: delta|done|failed, turnId, …}`, camelCase via `rename_all_fields`). **Images:** `codex exec --ephemeral -s workspace-write` in a scratch folder under the app cache, the CLI's `image_generation` tool, newest PNG = result (cannot be cancelled mid-flight). `DOODLE_CODEX_TRACE=1` prints every server line. Protocol schema for the installed version: `codex app-server generate-json-schema --out <dir>`. Each call carries Codex's preamble (~10–15k tokens). Verified 2026-09-21: `chatgpt · plus`, streaming, schema, images.

**Vision** — a character's or a place's picture goes with the words. Every character and location that feeds a writer or a generator and has a picture is attached to its request (`picturesFor()` in `jobs.ts`; rejected ones never), labelled so the model can tell which is whom, and the words say so (*Pictures attached, in order: 1. R-404 (a character)…*). A request keeps the document's own references (`Picture.ref`) so it can be read again; at run time `see()` finds each — the file for Codex, a 1024 copy of the bytes for a provider that takes them inline. Codex: a writer's turn carries them as `localImage` items on `turn/start`; an image run passes them to `codex exec` as `-i` files (before every other flag — `-i` takes several) with *keep who and where they show recognisable*; Rust's `stage()` sends an asset as its 1024 thumbnail and writes an in-memory picture to a scratch file. Ollama sends them (bare base64) only to a model that can see (`SEES`: llava, *-vl, gemma3, llama4…); the rest get the words that describe them. Provenance keeps what was shown (*Shown: R-404* under *Where this came from*). Checked against the service on 2026-09-23 through the app server with the fixture as "Mara": *"Mara is a black bear; background is blue."* **Give a picture**: drag a take out of a bloom onto a character or a place (it lights as the take passes over) and it is theirs — generate a look, give it, and every writer and generator it feeds sees it from then on (`giveLook`).

**Provenance** (`state/prov.ts`). Every generated thing records where it came from — provider, model, when, the prompt and system as they went out, the seed, what fed it (node, port and title as they were), the bible's rules, the run — keyed by **what it produced**: an image by its asset reference (content-addressed; `rekey()` follows them when the first save moves them into the folder), words by the run's id, which the writer and the pages it wrote carry as `node.from`. It saves inside `graph.json` (`provenance`) and comes back with the document. The inspector shows it as **Where this came from** for whatever is selected (`behind()` resolves through a node's take or its `from`): who and when, the seed, the inputs as rows that select them, and *what was asked* folded away.

**Remix, takes on the field, looks, frames** (`state/remix.ts`, `graph/looks.ts`) — Visual Electric's good ideas in a graph's terms (it spread a set of four on a canvas instead of a chat, offered one-click styles, and let you remix any image). **Remix** (a generated image's right-click menu, or *Where this came from*): a new generator appears under the one that made it, fed by the same things port for port, the same settings, that image's **seed held** and Fixed, its first take the image itself, and a Remix print beside it — a branch, the original untouched (`makerOf()` finds the generator by its outputs). **Pull a take out**: drag a take out of a bloom (past 6 px it follows the pointer as a small print, `.take-ghost`; a click still chooses it) and let go on the field — it is a card of its own there (`setOnField`, *Explore*, its record of where it came from with it) to lay beside the others. **Looks**: a style node starts from one of twelve (Marker, Risograph, Classic animation, 3D render, Airbrush, Stained glass, Watercolour, Film noir, Woodblock, Polaroid, Blueprint, Claymation) — the *Start from* pop-up fills description, palette and lighting, still yours to change (a `select` field's `fills`). **Frame**: a generator's shape is a choice (1:1 · 4:5 · 3:2 · 16:9 · 9:16, long side 1024, `FRAMES` in `kinds.ts`), not two steppers; an old graph's width and height become the nearest frame on load.

**Thumbnails** (`src-tauri/src/thumbs.rs`, `thumbFor()` in `state/assets.ts`, plan §10.2). Every asset gets a derivative per size — 256, 512, 1024 on the long side — made once by Rust off the main thread, kept in `thumbs/<hash>-<size>.<jpg|png>` beside `assets/` (a cache: never archived, remade if gone; transparency stays PNG; an image already small is its own). An image still in memory is drawn small by the webview. Bloom takes, faces and History read 256; a location's view, the contact sheet and media 512; a preview card 1024; the print on its page reads the original. The fixture bear: 1.6 MB → 76 KB on its card.

**Jobs** (`state/jobs.ts`). One at a time; `requestFor`/`textRequestFor` read through the wires (scene, then character, then style, then the bible); a generator renders `count` candidates (next seed along each), outputs to `assets/` via `write_asset` when the graph has a home, bloom under the rows, the take flows on; a **writer streams** (`job.partial`, shown on the page it feeds — card and document — as *arriving*, read-only, never the document until the end) and then **lays its words on pages** (`graph.layOnPages`: `paginate()` cuts at `PAGE_WORDS` 350 by paragraph, then by sentence; the page it feeds first, then the empty pages after it, then new pages made to the right — a page with its own words is never written over). The mock writer gives twelve paragraphs for `Length: Chapter`, one sentence for `Beat`; History (bar clock) lists runs, retries failed ones. Runs persist in `jobs.json`; in-flight at close → cancelled.

**Welcome** (`shell/Welcome.tsx`). The whole window before there is anything to look at, and on ⌘N: the DD mark, **Start** (the four templates) and **Lately** (the graphs this machine has opened — `recent()` keeps eight in `doodle.recent.v1`, a missing one is forgotten when picked) with *Open a graph…*. Escape closes it when something is already open. Only a strip at the title line carries `data-tauri-drag-region` — on the whole panel it **swallows the clicks** of everything inside it.

**Disk** (`state/doc.ts`, `src-tauri/src/commands.rs`). Save asks where once, then autosaves 600 ms after every journal entry; **Export as Markdown** (File › ⇧⌘E, ⌘K): the place you are in — the book, or the chapter — in reading order, chapters as headings, empty pages left out (`state/reading.ts` is the one order, shared with Read; `write_text` in Rust, atomic), then revealed in the Finder; **Archive Project… / Open Archive…** (File, ⌘K): the whole project as one `.doodlebox` — a zip of `manifest.json` (format, version, app, name, when, every file with its sha256 and size), `graph.json`, `jobs.json` and `assets/`, never `backups/`; an import checks every byte against the manifest **before writing anything**, refuses a changed or foreign file with a plain sentence, and opens what it wrote (`src-tauri/src/archive.rs`; `cargo test` covers the round trip, a tampered archive and a file that is not one); atomic writes; `backups/` before overwrite (≤1 per 10 min, newest 10); Duplicate Graph, Reveal in Finder; last graph restored on launch with its views. Assets hashed into `assets/<sha>.<ext>`; read back as data URLs (no asset-protocol scope); drops from Finder land on the card under the pointer or the open page. CSP set (`tauri.conf.json`; looser `devCsp`).

**The installed build** (PLAN.md M1.1–M1.3, M1.10's CI half). `pnpm release` (= `tauri build`) makes `Doodle.app` and `Doodle_<v>_aarch64.dmg` in `src-tauri/target/release/bundle/` — product name *Doodle*, id `com.x0bd.doodle`, macOS 13+, **ad-hoc signed** (`signingIdentity: "-"`, hardened runtime; no Apple account — D2), ~14 MB app, ~5 MB dmg. The version lives in `package.json` (the bundle reads it: `"version": "../package.json"`); `node scripts/version.mjs v0.2.0` writes a tag's version there and into `Cargo.toml`. `src-tauri/Info.plist` is merged into the bundle's: **a project is a document package** — `com.x0bd.doodle.project` conforms to `com.apple.package`, so `Name.doodle/` is one file in the Finder and double-click opens it; `com.x0bd.doodle.archive` is the `.doodlebox`. The types register with LaunchServices once the app is on disk, and from then on the open panel picks a project **as a file** (`pickOpenDir` filters on `doodle`; a directory picker would refuse a package). What the Finder hands over arrives as `RunEvent::Opened` (`opened.rs`) — often before the page listens — so the paths wait in Rust until the page takes them (`take_opened`; the `opened` event is only a nudge): at launch the handed-over project wins over the last one, and an archive is unpacked as *Open Archive* would. **File › Open Recent** is the platform's own submenu, filled by the page from `recent()` (`set_recent`; `recent.<n>`, `recent.clear` come back as menu ids). WebKit keeps the installed app's storage under `~/Library/WebKit/com.x0bd.doodle`, the dev binary's under `~/Library/WebKit/doodle` — separate recents, separate last graph. CI (`.github/workflows/`): `ci.yml` on master and PRs (check, vitest, cargo test, macOS 15); `release.yml` on a `v*` tag sets the version, tests, builds with `tauri-action` and puts the `.dmg` on a **draft, prerelease** GitHub Release carrying the *Open Anyway* instructions (also in the README).

**Providers** (Settings). Says what was found and what to do about what was not, with the line to type: Ollama *not installed* (ollama.com) / *not running* (installed at a known path — `ollama_where`; an app from the Finder has no shell PATH) / *nothing to write with* (`ollama pull qwen3:14b`) / ready with the writers and the rest by the names a person would say; ChatGPT *not found* (where it looked; `npm install -g @openai/codex@latest`, `codex login`) / *not signed in* / ready with the version and where it came from (npm, Homebrew, the ChatGPT app). **Look again** clears the status cache and re-scans for Codex (`codex_status` rescans; `find()` otherwise keeps its answer). About reads the bundle's version.

**The recovery log** (PLAN.md M1.4; `state/recovery.ts` the lines, `doc.ts` when, `recovery.rs` the disk). Autosave writes the graph whole but only when typing pauses (600 ms) — now also **at least every 5 s** while it does not — so between saves every change is appended as one JSON line, **fsynced, within 400 ms** of being made: whole values, never edits (a node as it now is or `null`, and the order, edges, bible, name, provenance only when they changed — the stores are immutable, so what changed is which objects are new). Replaying a line twice or a line the file already has changes nothing; a line the crash cut short is skipped. A project's log is `Name.doodle/recovery.log` (never archived, never duplicated); a graph that was never saved logs to Application Support `recovery/untitled.log` (`untitled-dev.log` for the dev binary, which shares the bundle id), beginning with a `base` line of the whole. A save clears the log and starts it again from the state it wrote (taken in the same moment as the file's words; appends, clears and reads go through one queue in order). **Opening** a project lays its log over it as one journal entry — *Recovered what was changed after the last save…* with **Undo** (the notice, `state/notice.ts` + `shell/Notice.tsx`: one sentence under the head, until answered); **launch** brings back a never-saved graph that was being worked on (*"…was never saved; here it is"*, **Save…**), after anything the Finder handed over and before the last graph (`launch()`, decided once per page). A card's measured height is not work: a log that holds nothing else is cleared, not offered. Leaving a document saves it at once if a save was pending. Checked 2026-09-23 with `kill -9` mid-typing (words every 0.3 s, so autosave never ran): the log held everything to 0.3 s before the kill, both for a project and for a never-saved graph, and both came back. **Found doing it:** `newGraph()`/`load()` reset the journal while `doc.path` was still the old project, the journal's subscriber took that for an edit, and 600 ms later the *new* graph was autosaved **into the old project** (New Graph overwrote the open project; opening B overwrote A). Now a reset is not an edit, the timer writes only the path it was set for if that is still the open document, and `write()` refuses otherwise.

**Versions** (PLAN.md M1.5; `state/versions.ts`, `shell/Versions.tsx`, `versions.rs`). A version is a node **and everything inside it** (a chapter: its pages and what they hold, with the wires between them) as it was, kept in the project: `versions/<id>.json` (written atomically) and `versions/index.jsonl` (a line each, appended and fsynced after the file is whole). Kinds kept: page, chapter, note, prompt, character, location, style, shot. **Daily**: after each save, what the save changed (`touched`: a node whose words, title, state, parent, pictures or tie changed — not its place or measured height — and every versioned place it is or was inside) is kept *as the save before it had it*, once a day per node (`due`), through a queue so two saves close together do not both take it — so yesterday's chapter is always there. **Kept**: File › Keep a Version (⌥⌘S) or ⌘K, of what is chosen, else the card being written in, else the page or chapter you are in (`versionTarget`). **Before a restore**: restoring keeps what is there first. **File › Versions…** (and ⌘K): the list down the left (Today 09:14 · Daily · words), the chosen one read against now with jsdiff's `diffWordsWithSpace` — what would come back wears the tint, what was written since is struck, spaces around a change left plain — a sentence saying what Restore would do, **Restore** (`restoreInto`: the version's nodes back, what was added inside since taken out, the node left where it now sits; one journal entry). A graph never saved has none (*Save…*). **Leaving a never-saved graph** that has been worked on now asks, as the platform does — *Keep "Untitled images"? It has never been saved.* **Save… / Don't Save / Cancel** (`mayLeave`, `askThree` = the dialog plugin's `message` with custom buttons) — on every way out: Open, Open Recent, the Finder's hand-over, the welcome; a project needs no question (leaving saves it). A launch that opens what the Finder handed over still says a kept never-saved graph is there (*…is kept* · **Open it**). **Found doing it:** (1) the body's `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` makes **WebKit drop a space that opens a new text run after a full stop** in our Inter (Chromium does not — the browser pane showed it fine); prose surfaces (`.pm`, `.prose-read`, `.draft-text`, `.vdiff`) now take proportional numbers; the WebKit harness (`wk.swift` in the scratchpad: a WKWebView snapshot of a test page with the app's CSS from the dev server) found it by bisection. (2) The **menu listener ran every item twice in development** — the second mount registered before the first had finished listening; it now awaits the one before (like `__doodleAgent`). (3) The native alert's buttons do not take synthetic clicks; `perform action "AXPress" of button n of window 1` (System Events, by pid) does.

**Integrity** (PLAN.md M1.6; `state/integrity.ts` + test, `integrity.rs` + tests). Every open goes through `check()`: not JSON, not a Doodle graph or no cards at all is **damaged** — the newest backup that passes opens instead (`list_backups`/`read_backup`, newest first), the damaged file kept beside the graph (`set_aside` → `graph-damaged-<secs>.json`), and a notice says so (*"…was damaged, so it opened from its backup of Wed 16:05…"*), then it saves sound again; a format **newer** than this Doodle is refused, never "repaired" (*Update Doodle to open it — nothing in it has been changed*); otherwise the file goes through the **migrations** (`FORMAT = 1`, `MIGRATIONS[n]` takes n to n + 1; a file with no version is 0) and is made whole — cards of a kind this Doodle does not know left out, missing parts filled in, a card inside one that is gone or inside itself put at the top, the order made to name every card once, wires to nothing taken out — each said (*"…was mended as it opened — the order of the cards was set right, and one wire to nothing was taken out"*) with the file as it was kept aside. **Tidy Unused Pictures…** (File, ⌘K): the pictures in `assets/` that nothing names — `graph.json`, `jobs.json`, every version, the recovery log — counted first (the dry run), then moved **to the Trash** (the `trash` crate; never deleted), their thumbnails with them, only on *Move to Trash*. **Notices queue** now (`notices`: the first shows with *1 of n*, each until answered; a new document clears them) — at launch the backup's notice and the kept-graph notice had covered each other. The native `ask` alert took a real CGEvent click here, though the three-button `message` one did not; neither is reliably in the AX tree.

**Journal** (`state/history.ts`). Snapshot entries; a drag is one, a run of typing is one (coalesce by key ≤1 s); undo/redo; selection survives. Edit menu items are Doodle's own so the journal gets them.

**Search.** ⌘K palette: nodes by title/kind/words + run, new, fit, the prompt bar, export, archive, open archive, settings; Enter frames, ⌘Enter opens. ⌘/ shortcuts sheet.

## Working

`pnpm tauri dev` (Rust ~1 min first time; `DOODLE_CODEX_TRACE=1 pnpm tauri dev` to watch Codex). The webview sometimes holds a half-applied HMR — `touch index.html` forces a reload. Capture only when Doodle is frontmost: front it with System Events, find the window by CoreGraphics (a 10-line Swift `win` listing `CGWindowListCopyWindowInfo` for owner `doodle`), `screencapture -R` its frame; keys via System Events `keystroke`; clicks via a CGEvent `click x y` (System Events `click at` is refused). The kit lives in the session scratchpad and is rebuilt in a minute: `win.swift` (prints `id x y w h` of doodle's window), `click.swift` (CGEvent down/up at a screen point), `shot.sh` (front, capture, downscale to 1000 px for reading); System Events can also click menu items by name (`tell menu bar item "File" … click menu item "New Graph"`). Drive the app read-only; leave file dialogs to the user. With the **installed app open too**, its window (often full screen) sits over the dev one and a click by coordinates lands in it: target by pid (`winp PID`, `shotp.sh PID`), and click only after checking the dev process is frontmost (`frontclick.sh PID X Y`). A Vite dependency re-optimisation reloads the page; the recovery log brings an unsaved graph back. A hot patch of `doc.ts` leaves a fresh `doc` store beside the old graph (*Untitled* over a saved project) — `touch index.html` for a clean page. `pnpm tauri dev -- -- <path.doodle>` opens a project at launch without a dialog. The web view has no working `window.confirm` (it answers no at once) — ask through `confirmAsk()` in `platform/fs.ts` (the dialog plugin's `ask`). `http://localhost:1430` in a browser works for pointer tests (⌘ keys mapped in `devKeys`); the Tauri `listen` error there is harmless; the browser tool's Return key does not reach the page — dispatch a KeyboardEvent instead. After HMR, Vite serves patched modules as `?t=…`, so `await import('/src/state/graph.ts')` from the console can be a **different store instance** from the running app's — drive through the DOM, not through imported stores. Synthetic `PointerEvent`s have no real pointer: `setPointerCapture` throws on them, so in handlers it goes **last and in a try** (the map and the width grip both died on it once). A selection change reaches React's `onSelect` via `document` `selectionchange`, not a `select` event.

## Taste, as settled with the user

- **Read the doctrine, not the screenshot.** References have been welcome (a modular synth panel, the Wavely node canvas), but taken literally they were rejected twice: a flat one-tone head with a cut-in plate ("ugly"), then a tactile panel of raised keys, inset wells and plates on every card ("the tactile plate does not work on the cards"). What held was translating them through `the-soft-machine.md`: nothing drawn, distance groups, Inter reads / mono reports, the tint for a chosen thing, capsules on controls.
- **Keep:** the role band on the head, the **lit dots** (bright, not the washed roles), the **amber leads** with plugs and jacks, cards that size to what they hold, the Outline, the welcome.
- **The user reacts to what they see in the real window.** Verify there (the capture kit), in both grounds, before calling a visual change done — the browser pane has hidden two real bugs (`.col` rows outside a pane; the drag region eating clicks).
- When the user says *revert*, revert exactly to the named state and keep only what they named.

## Known

- The mock writer repeats the brief before its paragraph; the mock image is always the same fixture, so repeated takes share a reference (keys are index-qualified for it).
- Outputs rendered before a graph is saved are data URLs until the first save moves them.
- The canvas shows assets at full resolution as data URLs; §12 wants thumbnails. Fine at tens of takes, not at hundreds.
- The Rust watcher sometimes misses an edit made by a script — `touch src-tauri/src/lib.rs` nudges it.
- The pane state once flipped to off/off during HMR + a process swap; never on a clean launch; store key is `doodle.ui.v1`. After many HMR patches the webview can hold stale handlers (keys, the wheel) — the fix is a relaunch, not code. Nothing may focus a field on arrival (the bar once did, and every field key went into the prompt). The menu listener no longer rots (it lives on the window).

The Keeper's Log

The fourteenth of March. Wind from the north-east, rising through the night, and the glass falling since noon. The lamp was lit at a quarter past six and burned clean until morning. At first light a ship was seen standing off the point with no sail set and no one at the wheel. She drifted on the tide towards the harbour mouth and grounded on the bar a little before eight. My daughter went down to the shore with the boatman, but they could not reach her for the sea. We shall try again at the turn of the tide. Oil used: three gallons. Wicks trimmed twice.
